# Benefits

## Health Insurance

The company covers 80% of the health insurance premium for the employee and 60% for enrolled dependents. New hires can enroll within 30 days of their start date; everyone else can enroll or make changes during open enrollment in November.

## Retirement

The company matches 50% of employee 401k contributions, up to 6% of salary contributed, for a maximum company match of 3% of salary.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of paid parental leave. Leave can be taken any time within the first year after the birth or adoption.

## Wellness and Education

A $50 monthly wellness stipend is available for gym memberships or fitness apps, submitted the same way as any other expense. Tuition reimbursement of up to $5,000 per year is available for job-related coursework, but requires manager pre-approval before the course begins.
