# Benefits

## Health Insurance

The company covers 80% of the employee's health insurance premium.

## Retirement (401k)

The company matches 50% of employee contributions, up to 6% of salary contributed. This means the maximum possible company match is 3% of salary.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave.

## Wellness Stipend

Employees receive a $50 monthly wellness stipend, submitted the same way as any other expense.

## Tuition Reimbursement

Tuition reimbursement requires pre-approval from the employee's manager before the course begins. This is a separate approval path from the department-head pre-approval required for general expenses over $500.
