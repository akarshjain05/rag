# Expense Policy

## Receipts and Reimbursement

Receipts are required for any individual expense over $50. Approved reimbursement requests are processed within two weeks through the finance portal. Expenses over $500 require pre-approval from the employee's department head before the cost is incurred.

## Travel Per Diem

For business travel, the meal per diem is $75 per day for domestic trips and $100 per day for international trips. Per diem covers meals only; lodging and transportation are reimbursed separately against actual receipts.

## Corporate Card

Employees who travel for business more than once per quarter are eligible for a corporate card, which removes the need for personal out-of-pocket spending on trips.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

Alcohol is not reimbursable except in the context of approved client entertainment. Personal entertainment, and traffic or parking fines incurred while on company business, are never reimbursable.
