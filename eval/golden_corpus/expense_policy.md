# Expense Policy

## Receipts

Receipts are required for any expense over $50.

## Meal Per Diem

The per diem for meals during domestic business travel is $75 per day.

## Pre-Approval

Expenses over $500 require pre-approval from a department head before they are incurred. There is no fixed turnaround time for this approval step.

## Reimbursement

Once an expense is approved, reimbursement is issued within two weeks through the finance portal.
