# Offboarding

## Access Revocation

All system access is revoked within 24 hours of an employee's last day, following the same urgency as a security incident under the security policy.

## Equipment Return

Company equipment must be returned within 5 business days of the last day. Remote employees are sent a prepaid shipping label so they can mail equipment back rather than deliver it in person.

## Exit Interview

HR schedules an exit interview with every departing employee. It is optional but strongly encouraged, since feedback from exit interviews directly informs retention efforts.

## Final Pay and Benefits

The final paycheck, including any accrued and unused vacation payout, is issued on the regular payroll schedule unless state law requires immediate payment. Health benefits continue through the end of the month in which the employee departs, and COBRA continuation paperwork is sent within 14 days of the last day.
