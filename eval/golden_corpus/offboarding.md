# Offboarding

## Equipment Return

Departing employees must return all company equipment within 5 business days of their last day.

## System Access

System access is revoked within 24 hours of an employee's last day -- the same urgency standard used for a suspected security incident.

## Final Pay and Vacation Payout

Any unused, accrued vacation, up to the 10-day carryover cap defined in the Vacation Policy, is paid out in the employee's final paycheck through payroll. This is separate from the finance portal, which is used only for expense reimbursements. The final paycheck is issued on the regular payroll schedule, unless state law requires it to be paid out sooner.

## Benefits

Health benefits continue through the end of the calendar month in which the employee departs, rather than ending immediately on the last day worked.
