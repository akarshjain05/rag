# Onboarding

## Before Day One

IT prepares accounts and equipment before a new hire's first day. For company laptops, this includes enabling full-disk encryption before the device is issued, per the Security Policy.

## Day One

New hires complete general orientation and meet their team.

## Day Two

New hires complete security awareness training, covering the requirements in the Security Policy (password rotation, minimum length, and mandatory MFA).

## First 30 Days

Code of conduct training and harassment prevention training must both be completed within the first 30 days. These trainings are administered by HR and Legal and are separate from the security awareness training in the Security Policy.

## 90-Day Check-In

At the 90-day mark, the new hire has a structured check-in with their manager to review ramp-up progress and set initial goals. This check-in is separate from the formal performance review cycle described in the Performance Reviews policy.
