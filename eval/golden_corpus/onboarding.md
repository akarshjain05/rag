# Onboarding

## Before Day One

IT provisions system access, including email, Slack, and GitHub, before the new hire's start date, so accounts are active on day one. Equipment requests are submitted to IT as soon as an offer is signed, so a laptop is ready and waiting on the first day.

## First Week

Day one includes HR orientation and introduction to an assigned onboarding buddy. Day two covers security training, drawing directly from the security policy. The rest of the first week is a mix of meet-the-team sessions and initial project ramp-up.

## Required Training

New hires must complete security awareness training, the code of conduct course, and harassment prevention training within their first 30 days.

## 90-Day Check-In

At the 90-day mark, new hires have a structured check-in with their manager to review ramp-up progress and set goals for the following quarter.
