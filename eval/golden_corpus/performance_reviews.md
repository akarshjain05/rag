# Performance Reviews

## Review Cycle

Formal performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Each employee submits a self-assessment one week before their review meeting.

## Calibration

Before ratings are finalized, leadership holds calibration sessions to compare ratings across teams and keep standards consistent company-wide.

## Promotions

Promotions are considered only during the December review cycle, so that they can be evaluated alongside calibrated, company-wide performance data.

## Performance Improvement Plans

An employee whose performance falls short of expectations may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP): a structured 60-day plan created by the manager with HR involvement, with formal check-ins at the 30-day and 60-day marks.
