# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility and Schedule

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Core hours are 10am-4pm in the employee's local timezone; outside of core hours, employees are free to set their own schedule as long as deliverables stay on track.

## Home Office Requirements

A dedicated workspace and a stable internet connection of at least 25 Mbps are required for remote work. New employees approved for regular remote work receive a one-time $500 home office equipment stipend, reimbursed through the same finance portal process described in the expense policy.

## International Remote Work

Working remotely from outside the employee's home country for more than 30 consecutive days requires advance approval from both HR and Legal, due to tax and employment-law implications. Requests must be submitted at least 60 days before the intended travel date.

## Security While Remote

All remote work must comply with the company security policy, including VPN use when accessing internal systems and full-disk encryption on any device used for work.
