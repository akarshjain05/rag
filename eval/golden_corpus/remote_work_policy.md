# Remote Work Policy

## Weekly Remote Days

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, with manager approval.

## Core Hours

Remote employees must be available during core hours of 10am-4pm in the employee's local timezone.

## Home Office Requirements

Remote employees must maintain a dedicated home workspace and an internet connection of at least 25 Mbps. These requirements must be in place before an employee's first day of remote work, independent of the account and equipment provisioning handled during onboarding.

## Home Office Stipend

Remote employees receive a one-time $500 stipend to set up their home office. This stipend is reimbursed through the same finance-portal process used for any other business expense.

## Security Requirements for Remote Work

Remote employees must connect through the company VPN whenever accessing internal systems from outside the office.

## Working Internationally

Employees who plan to work internationally for more than 30 consecutive days must provide at least 60 days of advance notice and receive approval from both HR and Legal before departing.
