# Security Policy

## Password Requirements

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and must be rotated every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all employee accounts.

## Device Encryption

Full-disk encryption must be enabled on any device used for work, including company laptops. For company-issued laptops, encryption must be enabled before the device is issued to the employee.

## Lost or Stolen Devices

A lost or stolen device must be reported to IT Security within 4 hours of discovery.

## Security Incident Reporting

Any suspected security incident must be reported within 1 hour of discovery.
