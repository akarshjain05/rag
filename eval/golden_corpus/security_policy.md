# Security Policy

## Device Security

All company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled before they are issued. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and rotated every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every company system, with no exceptions for convenience.

## Network Access

Employees must connect through the company VPN whenever accessing internal systems from outside the office network. Split-tunneling is disabled by default on the VPN client.

## Incident Reporting

Suspected security incidents, including phishing attempts and unusual account activity, must be reported to security@ within one hour of discovery. A lost or stolen device must be reported within four hours so IT can trigger a remote wipe before the encryption key can be compromised.

## Data Classification

Internal documents are classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Confidential and Restricted material may not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage under any circumstances.
