# Vacation Policy

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue paid vacation at a rate of 1.5 days per month, which works out to 18 days per year. Part-time employees who work fewer than 30 hours per week accrue vacation on a prorated basis, at 0.75 days per month.

## Carryover

Unused vacation carries over into the next calendar year up to a maximum of 10 days. Any accrued balance beyond that cap is forfeited on January 1st. There is no exception process for extending the carryover cap.

## Requesting Time Off

Vacation requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal and require manager approval. During the last two weeks of each fiscal quarter, no more than two people from the same team may be on vacation at the same time, to keep quarter-end work covered.

## Payout on Termination

When an employee leaves the company, any unused accrued vacation (up to the 10-day carryover cap) is paid out in the final paycheck at the employee's regular daily rate.
