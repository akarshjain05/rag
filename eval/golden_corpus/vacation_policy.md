# Vacation Policy

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue vacation days at a rate of 1.5 days per month. Part-time employees who work fewer than 30 hours per week accrue vacation at a rate of 0.75 days per month.

## Carryover

Unused vacation days carry over into the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 10 days. Any unused balance beyond 10 days is forfeited at year-end.

## Requesting Vacation

Vacation requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal. Requests submitted with less notice may be denied at manager discretion.

## Payout on Departure

See the Offboarding policy for how unused, accrued vacation is handled when an employee leaves the company.
